# 14 · 🏆 Famous games & legendary sidelines

[Back to README](../README.md)

Two things every chess lover should know: **the games that made history**, and the
**special deviations from standard openings that became main lines** because a strong
player made them famous.

## ⭐ Sidelines / deviations that became popular main lines

| Sideline | Parent opening | Key moves | Origin | Links |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Marshall Attack** | Ruy Lopez | 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5 a6 4.Ba4 Nf6 5.O-O Be7 6.Re1 b5 7.Bb3 d6 8.c3 O-O **8...d5!?** | Marshall–Capablanca, New York 1918 — a shock sacrifice of a pawn that became a top main line | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Attack) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Ruy_Lopez_Marshall_Attack) |
| **Berlin Defense / Berlin Endgame** | Ruy Lopez | 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5 Nf6 | Popularized at the very top by Kramnik–Kasparov, World Championship 2000 — the "drawing weapon" that turned 3...Nf6 into the main line | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Defense) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Ruy_Lopez_Berlin_Defense) |
| **Najdorf Variation** | Sicilian Defense | 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 d6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 **a6** | Named after Miguel Najdorf; the sharpest and most popular Sicilian at master level | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicilian_Defence,_Najdorf_Variation) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Sicilian_Defence_Najdorf_Variation) |
| **Sveshnikov Variation** | Sicilian | 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 cxd4 4.Nxd4 Nf6 5.Nc3 **e5** | Modern main line, famous for razor-sharp play | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicilian_Defence,_Sveshnikov_Variation) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Sicilian_Defence_Sveshnikov_Variation) |
| **Dragon Variation** | Sicilian (Accelerated) | 1.e4 c5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.d4 g6 (…d6, …Bg7, …O-O, h5 attack) | The Yugoslav attack against it made it legendary | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Variation) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Sicilian_Defence_Dragon_Variation) |
| **Grünfeld Defense** | Indian defenses | 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 g6 3.Nc3 **d5** | Hypermodern counterattack against the center | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gr%C3%BCnfeld_Defence) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Grunfeld_Defence) |
| **Benko (Volga) Gambit** | Queen's Gambit / Indian | 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 c5 3.d5 **b5!?** | Pawn gambit that leads to wild, unbalanced play | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benko_Gambit) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Benko_Gambit) |
| **London System** | 1.d4 systems | 1.d4 d5 2.**Bf4** | Not a "deviation" in the classical sense, but a system that became a top-3 most-played line among amateurs | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_System) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/London_System) |
| **Catalan Opening** | Queen's Gambit | 1.d4 Nf6 2.c4 e6 3.g3 | Solid + pressure; a modern main line | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_Opening) · [lichess](https://lichess.org/opening/Catalan_Opening) |

## ♟ Games with special historical significance

| Game | Year | What was special | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Immortal Game** — Anderssen vs. Kieseritzky | 1851 | Anderssen sacrificed queen, both rooks and a bishop for a mating attack | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immortal_Game) |
| **Evergreen Game** — Anderssen vs. Dufresne | 1852 | Model attack: 15.Ne4! and a queen sacrifice ending in mate | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evergreen_Game) |
| **Opera Game** — Morphy vs. Duke of Brunswick & Count Isouard | 1858 | Blindfold-friendly miniature: 16-move mate in the Paris Opera | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opera_game) |
| **Game of the Century** — Fischer vs. Byrne | 1956 | 17-year-old Fischer's queen sacrifice (17...Bxf2+! … 23...Qf6!!) and 26-move demolition | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game_of_the_Century_(chess)) |
| **AlphaZero vs. Stockfish match** | 2017/2018 | Engine games with "alien" piece play (e.g. the bishop pair, king safety sacrifices) that changed how humans think about initiative | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AlphaZero) |
| List of famous chess games | — | Wikipedia's curated list of notable games | [wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_chess_games) |
