# 1 · Play & learn online

[Back to README](../README.md)

Free places to play and train. All link-only — accounts are created on the original sites.

| Resource | What you get | Access / license | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| lichess | Free, ad-free, open-source server: unlimited games, puzzles, analysis, studies, tournaments | Free & open source (AGPL-3.0) | https://lichess.org/ |
| lichess — learn (interactive basics) | Step-by-step lessons from beginner to intermediate | Free | https://lichess.org/learn |
| lichess — studies & puzzle training | Community studies, themed puzzles, puzzle storm/rush | Free | https://lichess.org/study · https://lichess.org/training |
| Chess.com | Games, bots, lessons, videos (free tier) | Free tier — content link-only | https://www.chess.com/ |
| Chess.com Lessons | Structured video/text courses | Free tier | https://www.chess.com/lessons |
| FICS — Free Internet Chess Server | Classic free server, also hosts a huge public game archive | Free & open | https://www.freechess.org/ |
| Internet Chess Club (ICC) | Strong-player pool, events | Free tier available | https://www.chessclub.com/ |
| PlayChess (ChessBase) | Play vs. humans and engines | Free tier available | https://www.playchess.com/ |
