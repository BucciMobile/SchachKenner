## 📊 Most important & most played openings

Stats from **Lichess rated games, Sept 2026, players 1600–1999** (28.7M games):
White opened **1.e4 in 57 %** and **1.d4 in 27 %** of games (84 % together). Against 1.e4
Black most often played **1...e5**, then the **Sicilian 1...c5**, **Scandinavian 1...d5**,
**French 1...e6** and **Caro-Kann 1...c6**. Across all 2.8 billion rated Lichess games,
the **Sicilian Defense is the single most played opening (~10 %)**, followed by the
Scandinavian, French, Italian Game and Caro-Kann; the **London System** is a top-3
most-played line among amateurs. (Sources: Lichess opening statistics; see also
[chessvia](https://www.chessvia.ai/openings), [openingspy](https://openingspy.com/guides/most-popular-chess-openings).)
