# 9 · Books & learning material

[Back to README](../README.md)

> **Copyright rule:** we host **only public-domain books** (first published ≤ 1929, most with an explicit
> *Public Domain Mark 1.0* on the Internet Archive — proofs in
> [`licenses/books-public-domain.md`](../licenses/books-public-domain.md)). Modern copyrighted books are
> **link-only** (publisher / library). We deliberately do **not** list "free book — you only pay shipping"
> offers or shops that resell public-domain scans as if they were their own product.

## 📚 Hosted in this repo — 17 PD chess books in 8 languages

Full texts in [`data/books/`](../data/books/) (index with SHA-256 per file), grouped by language:
**English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Русский, Português, Svenska.**
Highlights: Nimzowitsch *My System* (1925), Lasker *Manual of Chess* (1925 ed.), Capablanca *Chess
Fundamentals* (1921), Staunton *Chess-Player's Companion* (1849), Philidor *Analyse du jeu des échecs*,
Bláthy's mate-in-many problems (1890), Petrov's *Šachmatnaja igra* (1824, first Russian chess book), …

> 💡 **More languages:** chess notation (SAN) is universal, but books are easier in your own language.
> Search the Internet Archive with the native word for chess — German *Schach*, French *échecs*,
> Spanish *ajedrez*, Italian *scacchi*, Russian *шахматы*, Portuguese *xadrez*, Dutch *schaak*,
> Polish *szachy* — e.g. `https://archive.org/search?query=schach` and filter `licenseurl:publicdomain`.
> Project Gutenberg's chess shelf: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/subject/1677 (PD ebooks).

## ⚠️ Scam warning

Offers like *"free chess book, you only pay €9.95 shipping"* or shops selling reprints of 19th-century
public-domain books as proprietary products are **not listed here**. If a book is genuinely public domain
or CC-licensed, the full text is legally free — no shipping required.
