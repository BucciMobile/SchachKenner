About the FICS Games Database

Welcome to the FICS Games Database, the world's largest chess database. It has the games played and relayed on the Free Internet Chess Server. The database is updated in real time: you can find and download games right after they are finished. When you are logged on to FICS, you can also use the database through GameBot.

Searching

If you leave the names of both players empty, you see the most recent games finished on FICS. All dates and times are FICS server time: US Eastern time (EST/EDT) since December 5, 2014, US Pacific time (PST/PDT) before. For fast searches, see the search hints.

Data

GameBot has collected all rated and unrated games played on FICS since October 2008, except bughouse games and games played by or against unregistered guests. Bughouse games are in the Bughouse database. woof contributed rated lightning games between human players (with move times) since March 2005. marcelk contributed rated blitz, lightning and standard games since November 1999 (without move times). Move times are available for all games since November 2008.

The opening explorer is based on all games with an average player rating of 2000 or more. If you leave Average rating on Any, you see the numbers for all of these games. New games are added to the opening explorer every day.
