Compatibility: Scid vs. PC and Scid 5.x

Currently, two different versions of the software are widely used. The tried-and-true Scid v.s PC/MAC uses the *.si4 database format, which is limited to a maximum of 16,777,216 games. Scid 5.x released in 2023, on the other hand, uses the new *.si5 format and can easily manage several billion games. Simply download the database that matches the version you have installed.

Monthly Updates In addition to the complete main databases, you’ll also find our monthly update files here. These files contain a concise compilation of all new games added since the database’s last major release.

Instructions for Extracting (.7z)

The downloads are compressed. To unzip the files on Windows, you’ll need 7-Zip or a similar program. On a Mac, for example, you can easily unzip them using Keka.

Important Note for Windows 11

Die Verwendung von nicht-lateinischen Zeichen (z. B. Arabisch oder Kyrillisch) in Dateipfaden kann unter Windows 11 zu Problemen beim Entpacken von Archiven führen. Bitte achte darauf, für den Entpack-Vorgang ausschließlich Verzeichnisse mit lateinischen Zeichen in den Ordner- und Dateinamen zu verwenden.
