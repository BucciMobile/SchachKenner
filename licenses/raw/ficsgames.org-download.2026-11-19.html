  • The database has the games played on FICS since November 1999.
  • Collections are bzip2 archives (.pgn.bz2); a player's games come as a zip file. Both can be unpacked with 7-Zip.
  • Collections are available for finished months and years; a player's games and titled players' games for whole years.
  • Move times are available for games played since October 2008, as are variant games (wild, crazyhouse etc.).
  • RDs of both players are in the PGN headers since August 16, 2015.
  • Up to 5,000 games of any search can be downloaded from the search results.
  • The database contains a few games from before November 1999, you can download them here.
